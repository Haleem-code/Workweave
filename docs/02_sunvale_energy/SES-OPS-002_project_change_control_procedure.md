# Sunvale Energy Systems Project Change Control Procedure

Document ID: SES-OPS-002
Version: 1.0
Effective: 2026-10-01
As of: 2026-10-15
Classification: Fictional company test fixture
Owner: Mariam Okeke

## Purpose and scope

This procedure applies to schedule and commercial changes on Sunvale installation projects. Ibrahim Sani records a proposed change before implementation. The change log must identify the site, reason, cost impact, schedule impact, and supporting supplier or customer evidence.

## Approval thresholds

Programme Director Mariam Okeke may approve a change with an incremental cost of up to NGN 1,000,000 if the programme completion date is unchanged. Managing Director Chika Nwosu must approve any change above NGN 1,000,000 or any change that moves the programme completion date. Exactly NGN 1,000,000 remains within Mariam Okeke's authority when the completion date is unchanged.

## Customer approval

A customer must accept a revised contractual site handover date before the project team records it as agreed. Internal approval alone is not customer acceptance. Keep requested, internally approved, and customer accepted dates as separate fields.

## Dependency review

Procurement records confirmed equipment arrival dates. Engineering checks whether an equipment delay reaches the critical path. A late item that is not needed until after its revised arrival should remain a monitored dependency rather than an overdue handover.

## Reporting and evidence

Ibrahim Sani updates the change log within one business day of receiving a supplier revision. The weekly project report must distinguish completed, blocked, and planned work. Include the source date and approver for each approved change. A quote request does not establish a purchase commitment.
