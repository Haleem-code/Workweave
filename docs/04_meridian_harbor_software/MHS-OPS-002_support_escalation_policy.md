# Meridian Harbor Software Support Escalation Policy

Owner: Ada Bello

## Purpose and scope

This policy sets internal support handling targets for TeamDesk and LaunchPath. It applies to active customer support cases. Severity is based on the reported business effect and must be checked against current case evidence.

## Severity one

Severity one means all production users of an affected customer are unable to use a core workflow and no workaround is available. The target first response is 30 minutes during the support window. Engineering Lead Daniel Eze owns technical escalation. The target is for first response, not a guaranteed resolution time.

## Severity two

Severity two means a core workflow is degraded or some users are blocked while a workaround remains available. The target first response is four support hours. Customer Success Lead Ada Bello owns customer coordination. Engineering owns technical diagnosis and records the next update time.

## Support window

The standard support window is 08:00 to 18:00 Africa/Lagos, Monday through Friday. Support hour targets count only that window. The company has no documented standard weekend response target in this test policy. Do not describe business hour targets as round the clock guarantees.

## Release communication

Customer Success sends a release notice after Engineering confirms the release and the Managing Director approves the customer communication. A planned release date is not confirmation that the release has shipped. Record pilot, planned release, and generally available as different product states.

## Evidence and closure

Keep case ID, customer, severity rationale, owner, source time, and the next update in the case record. Close the case only after the agreed outcome is confirmed. MHS-RPT-003 provides the October release baseline. No rule in this document grants an assistant access to production systems or authority to message customers.
