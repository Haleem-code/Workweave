# Cedargrain Foods Lot Hold and Release Procedure

Version: 1.0
Effective: 2026-10-01
As of: 2026-10-15
Classification: Fictional company test fixture
Owner: Bisi Adeyemi

## Purpose and scope

This procedure records internal approval responsibilities for held finished goods. It is a fictional administrative workflow for testing. It does not provide laboratory methods, technical food safety criteria, or regulatory instructions.

## Creating a hold

A production supervisor records the lot ID, product ID, quantity, reason, time, and physical storage location. Held packs must be excluded from available to promise quantities in planning records. A hold applies to the identified lot; do not mark unrelated lots as held without separate evidence.

## Release authority

Only Quality Manager Bisi Adeyemi may approve release of a held lot. Production Manager Femi Oladele may supply production records and request a review but cannot approve release. An unresolved review remains on hold even when a customer deadline is close.

## Evidence and status

A release entry must identify the lot, reviewer, review date, supporting records, and approved disposition. Pending, released, and rejected are distinct states. A promise to complete a review is not a release decision. A missing decision should be reported as unknown or pending rather than inferred from silence.

## Customer allocation

Before confirming dispatch, planning checks the lot status and quantity available. If an allocation depends on a held lot, record the customer order and ask the account owner to prepare a revised plan. Customer communication follows the account approval process; the hold record itself is not permission to send a message.

## Reporting cadence

Femi Oladele sends the open hold register to Bisi Adeyemi each business day by 10:00 Africa/Lagos. Keep the last confirmed status visible until an authorized change is recorded. Use CGF-RPT-003 for the test scenario dated 2026-10-15.
