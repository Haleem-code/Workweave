# KoraBridge Logistics Delivery Exception Procedure

Document ID: KBL-OPS-002
Version: 1.0
Effective: 2026-10-01
As of: 2026-10-15
Classification: Fictional company test fixture
Owner: Ada Bello

## Purpose and scope

Use this procedure when a confirmed supplier delay could prevent a customer dispatch. It applies to scheduled deliveries handled by the Lagos distribution hub and the Ibadan cross dock. Assess the required dispatch date, usable stock, and confirmed incoming quantity before reporting an order as at risk.

## Triage and evidence

Tunde Akin checks the purchase order, the supplier confirmation, the customer order, and usable stock. Link every finding to an order ID and a dated source. A late inbound shipment alone does not prove that every customer order is affected. Record which quantity is covered by stock and which quantity depends on the delayed delivery.

## Escalation and timing

If a customer dispatch is at risk within five calendar days, Tunde Akin must notify Operations Director Ada Bello within two business hours of validating the risk. The clock starts when the risk is confirmed during operating hours, 08:00 to 17:00 Africa/Lagos, Monday through Friday. Risks confirmed outside those hours enter the queue at 08:00 on the next business day. This is an internal escalation rule, not a customer response promise.

## Approvals and ownership

Ada Bello approves revised dispatch plans. Zainab Sule approves any incremental spend above NGN 250,000. The procurement lead obtains a written revised delivery date from the supplier. A review task should identify the responsible person, affected order, evidence, and next checkpoint. Creating a task does not mean a customer has been contacted.

## Customer communication and closure

The account manager drafts a customer update only after Ada Bello approves the revised plan. Record approval and the eventual customer response separately. Close the exception after the revised dispatch is confirmed or an approved alternative is completed. Keep the original and revised supplier dates in the exception record.
